# nDashMo short: three storylines

The one thing: an old Android tablet becomes a car head unit that knows when the car is on.
Remember (viewer's words): "it wakes when I start the car and sleeps when I stop."
Spine: one drive, from plugging in to unplugging.

Assumptions: 30-40 s, vertical 1080x1920 (Reels / Shorts / TikTok), music and sound effects only (free:
synthesized in the project, no paid voice or catalog music), no voiceover. Screens come from the real pages
(launcher-prototype/lite.html and glass.html). Car shots (cable going in, the tablet in the dash) are the
user's footage; until then they are labelled placeholders.

## A. Car on, car off (recommended: a moment everyone with a car knows)

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take that follows the tablet through one drive.
The dark tablet in the dash; a cable clicks in and nDashMo wakes, the music picking up where it stopped. The
drive plays out on the screen, and at the end the cable comes out: the music pauses, the trip goes to 0.0 and
the screen sleeps by itself.

- First 2 s: a black tablet screen in the dash, a USB-C plug pushes in, and on the click the screen lights onto
  the Matte dashboard. Title: "Plug in. It's on."
- Beats: plug in, wake -> music resumes, the album art reel turns -> map tilts into the driving view, speed
  climbs -> dusk: Light turns to Dark, Matte morphs into Glass -> arrive, unplug: pause, trip 0.0, screen off
  -> end card: the icon carried in, "nDashMo", "Free APK on GitHub".
- Look: Matte Light's own ground (#dfe1e4) and panels, Geist type, the accent #007AFF only on the payoff and
  the end card; the material is the tablet itself.
- Sound: a soft synth pulse that starts on the click and stops dead on the unplug; plug click, wake tone.

## B. Fifty apps out

Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one.
The stock tablet is a wall of icons nobody asked for; a counter takes them off one by one from the real removal
list until the screen is empty, and the empty screen builds itself into nDashMo panel by panel.

- First 2 s: a cluttered One UI home screen, the counter "50" in huge type, the first icons popping off.
- Beats: clutter -> 50 to 0 with real package names flying past -> empty screen -> rail, map, clock, car and
  music panels drop in -> the dashboard alive -> "Same tablet. Now a head unit." -> end card.
- Look: One UI's own home as the "before" world, Matte Dark as the "after" world, giant condensed numerals
  (Sofia Sans Extra Condensed, the car panel's speed face).
- Sound: a tick per removed app that speeds up, then silence, then the beat drops with the dashboard.
- Needs: a screenshot of the old home screen (placeholder until then).

## C. Built by asking (the bold one)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
The dashboard stays in the middle of the frame the whole time; the maker's real messages to Claude appear
one by one, and each one changes the dashboard in front of you.

- First 2 s: a chat bubble "can we create the homescreen like CarPlay?" over an empty tablet outline, which
  snaps into the first dashboard.
- Beats: "make it liquid glass" -> Glass -> "the red doesn't stand out" -> warnings go strong red -> "invert
  the flip clock in light mode" -> clock flips dark -> "heading south but the arrow says north" -> compass
  shows "-" until GPS knows -> "Built by asking. Free on GitHub." -> end card.
- Look: the chat bubbles in plain system type on Matte's grey; the dashboard is the one constant object.
- Sound: a soft key-tap per message, one musical hit per change.
- Note: the messages are the maker's own words (lightly cleaned for spelling), not invented.
