## What you film (phone, landscape, 4K or 1080p, daylight, engine off is fine)

1. The push-in from the driver's seat to the tablet in the dash (scene 1), slow, about 10 s. Two or three takes.
2. A hand reaching for a small icon at the top of the old home screen (scene 2), 5 s.
3. A thumb tapping the rail, then a two-finger swipe for volume (scene 4), 10 s.
4. Optional: a few seconds of the road through the windscreen for the background of scene 5.

Tips: wipe the screen, turn tablet brightness to full, avoid the sun on the glass, hold the phone steady
(lean on the seat), no shots while you drive.
