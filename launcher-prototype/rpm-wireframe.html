Car panel, OBD: engine speed card

Sample reading: 48 km/h, 2,528 rpm, coolant 88°, fuel 62%, battery 14.2 V. Each panel is Lite's real Car panel size on the tablet (270 x 304 px), shown at 150%. Numbers use Sofia Sans Extra Condensed (already bundled) for the digital-cluster feel.

HUD speedometer (first photo)

  • What works: one huge white number, so speed reads in a glance. The arc is a bar of separate segments that fills up, which reads faster than a needle.
  • What to skip: four colours (white, blue, yellow, red) and clock plus distance digits all at the same weight. It is busy for a 270 px card.

Toyota digital cluster (second photo)

  • What works: one colour on black. The tach is a row of bars that fills left to right with 0 to 6 above it, and the fuel gauge is a short stack of blocks. Both read as "how full", with no precise reading needed.
  • What to skip: tiny trip and scale labels; on the tablet they would fall under the 12 px floor.

Closest to the Toyota layout: speed and the tach strip on the left, fuel as a column of 8 blocks on the right (the bottom two are dark red when empty and turn red only when fuel is down to them). Coolant and battery get two bigger cards since fuel moved out.

In the app all three cost about the same: the segments are drawn once, and each OBD reading (about twice a second) only switches which ones are lit, with no easing in between, as the rule for Lite says. Glass would get the same layout on its glass panel.
