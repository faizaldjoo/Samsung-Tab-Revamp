Car panel, GPS: gauge card

For the tablet's GPS (OBD comes later). GPS gives speed, heading, trip, drive time and average, so the segment bar shows speed. Sample: 48 km/h heading NE 42°, trip 12.4 km, 0:38 driving, average 31 km/h. Each panel is Lite's real Car panel size on the tablet (270 x 304 px), shown at 150%. Numbers use Sofia Sans Extra Condensed (already bundled).

HUD speedometer (first photo)

  • What works: one huge white number, so speed reads in a glance. The arc is a bar of separate segments that fills with speed, which reads faster than a needle. The compass rose sits apart, in a corner.
  • What to skip: four colours (white, blue, yellow, red) and clock plus distance digits as bold as the speed. Too busy for a 270 px card.

Toyota digital cluster (second photo)

  • What works: one colour on black. The bar gauge fills left to right under a short scale, so it reads as "how much" without a precise reading.
  • What to skip: tiny trip and scale labels; on the tablet they would fall under the 12 px floor.

The HUD's layout: speed on the left, a compass rose in the corner (it turns so N points north; the blue arrow in the middle is where the car heads), the same speed strip below.

In the app all three cost about the same: the bars, tape and rose are drawn once, and each GPS fix (once a second) only switches which bars are lit and shifts or turns the compass, with no easing in between, as the rule for Lite says. With OBD later, the same bar can show engine rpm and fuel can get its own gauge. Glass would get the same layout on its glass panel.
