Controls, redone

Dashflow at the tablet's real page size, in Matte. Today's Controls puts 15 different kinds of thing in one grid of identical cards. Both options sort them by job: quick switches, how the screen behaves, and the app itself (the new About card with the highlights and a button to the full details).

Option A: full screen, three columns

Controls becomes a page like All apps, the rail stays. Quick on the left (big tiles, the first thing a hand reaches), Display and driving in the middle as plain rows, Dashflow on the right: version, what's new, what needs turning on, battery and heat, and Details. Everything is visible at once, nothing scrolls. In Glass the middle column also holds Glass effect and Wallpaper.

Controls stays a drawer beside the rail, so the map and player stay in view, but shows one group at a time: Quick, Display, Dashflow. Calmer per screen and bigger targets, at the cost of one extra tap to reach a setting in another tab. Shown here on the Dashflow tab.
