Car panel under the map

Lite's home screen at the tablet's size (1340 x 800). The Car panel (dashed) moves into the map's column, under the map. Its tall slot today is mostly empty space between the speed bar and the trip numbers, so it only needs about a third of the height. The two options differ in what takes the freed top-right space.

Now

Map in its own full-height column; the Car panel shares the top row with Today and is 514 px tall for about 250 px of content.

Option A: wide Today

Left column: map 500 px, Car 270 px (same content, packed: speed and compass in one row, the bar, the three numbers). Top right: Today across the full width, the clock and the weather side by side, so both get bigger. The music row and sliders stay as they are.

Option B: bigger player

Left column the same as A. Right side: Today on top, the player under it, both full width, and the sliders become one tall column on the far right. The player gets a taller panel with room for a bigger cassette and controls; the clock shrinks a little.

Trade-offs

Map: about 36% shorter (780 to 500 px), so less road shows ahead of the car; the car moves up to keep it at 75% of the height. The turn card and ETA bar still fit. Car panel: loses nothing, only empty space. Glass: the same grid change works there too.
